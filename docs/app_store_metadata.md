## App Description

### English (Primary for Apple Review)

**Short description (80자):**
Discover your guardian animal and explore personality insights through ancient Eastern wisdom.

**Full description:**
Meet your AI guardian animal! Based on the 12 zodiac animals of East Asian tradition, SaDam matches you with a unique AI character that understands your personality.

**What makes SaDam unique:**
- 12 zodiac animal characters with distinct personalities
- Conversational AI that adapts to your personality profile
- 60 unique character combinations (5 elements x 12 animals)
- Beautiful animations and immersive onboarding experience
- Personality insights based on 1,000+ years of Eastern wisdom

**How it works:**
1. Meet this year's guardian animal in our interactive onboarding
2. Enter your birth details through a fun chat conversation
3. Discover YOUR guardian animal with a stunning reveal animation
4. Chat with your animal companion for personality insights

**Features:**
- 12 AI zodiac characters (Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Sheep, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig)
- MBTI-style personality analysis
- Daily personality tips
- Compatibility analysis with friends and family
- Available in 17 languages
